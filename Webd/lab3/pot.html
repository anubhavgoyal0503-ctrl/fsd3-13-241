From Mitti to Matka

Long before refrigerators and plastic bottles, Indian households relied on the humble matka to store their drinking water.

Potters shape natural clay, dry it and fire it to create an earthen vessel that is both functional and beautiful.

Today, the matka represents more than just a water pot — it represents sustainability, simplicity and a connection with our roots.
